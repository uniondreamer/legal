Local by default. No account, advertising, tracking, or document upload during normal app use. Email support is optional.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy describes the current Folio macOS app and this support and legal website. Folio is a local Markdown reader and editor. Normal reading, editing, search, and export do not send your documents to the publisher.

The app has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking pixels, or app-operated cloud synchronization. The optional feedback email described below is separate from normal app use.

2. Information kept on your Mac

  • Documents and exports. Files are read and saved in the locations you choose. Folder search processes the selected workspace locally, including unsaved text in open documents.
  • Preferences. Folio stores language, appearance, font and focus preferences, first-launch state, and the selected workspace folder path on your Mac.
  • macOS records. macOS may maintain autosaved document versions, recent-file history, window state, and system diagnostic records under its own settings.

Markdown files and exports are ordinary files. Folio does not add its own password protection or file encryption. Your device security, macOS permissions, backup settings, and any disk encryption you enable govern protection of those files.

3. Rendering, links, and other services

Markdown rendering libraries, equation fonts, and diagram rendering are bundled with Folio. Remote images are blocked in the preview. The preview uses a nonpersistent WebKit data store.

If you open an external link, your browser or email app handles it. If you save documents in an iCloud Drive folder or another synchronized location, that service may upload the files according to your settings. Folio does not operate those services.

Apple or a download provider may process distribution, purchase, or diagnostic information under its own policies and your settings. This policy describes Folio’s own behavior; it does not replace those providers’ policies.

4. Optional feedback and support email

For feedback or privacy questions, email yfeiib@gmail.com. Clicking an email link opens your configured email application; nothing is sent until you choose to send a message.

If you write to this address, the recipient receives your sender address, any display name your email app includes, message contents, attachments, and ordinary email delivery metadata. This information is used to answer your request, investigate a reported issue, and manage the correspondence. Sending feedback is optional and is not required to use Folio.

The support mailbox uses Google’s Gmail service, and your own email provider also processes the message. Their processing and international storage practices are governed by their respective terms and privacy policies. Folio itself does not attach documents or diagnostics automatically. Please remove private material before sending examples or screenshots.

Support correspondence is kept for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, related follow-up, or applicable recordkeeping obligations. You may use the same address to request access, correction, or deletion of correspondence, subject to applicable law.

5. Sharing, advertising, and transfers

Folio does not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or create advertising profiles. Normal document use does not create a publisher-held document repository.

Information you voluntarily send by email is processed through the email providers described above. It may also need to be disclosed where a binding legal obligation requires it. No local document is automatically sent to support, an advertiser, or a data broker.

6. Your choices, retention, and deletion

You control where to save, copy, export, or delete your documents. Close Folder stops the current workspace and clears Folio’s remembered workspace path. Other preferences remain until changed or removed. Files, backups, synchronized copies, and macOS document history may remain after uninstalling the app; manage those separately through macOS and your storage providers.

Depending on applicable law and the information actually held, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, obtain a copy of, restrict, or object to processing of personal information. Send privacy requests about support correspondence to yfeiib@gmail.com. Do not send a government ID unless a lawful and necessary verification method has been agreed.

Folio cannot retrieve or erase files that only exist on your device or with a storage provider you selected. If local law provides a right to complain to a privacy regulator or seek a remedy, these documents do not limit that right.

7. Children’s information

Normal offline app use does not ask for an age, child profile, or account and does not include behavioral advertising. Do not send children’s personal information in support messages. A parent or guardian may contact the feedback address about a message that includes a child’s information.

8. This website and offline copies

These HTML files contain no JavaScript, cookies, browser storage, forms, remote fonts, analytics, or automatically loaded third-party assets. Opening them from your device does not contact a website. Relative links open the other files in this bundle.

The online version is prepared for GitHub Pages hosting. When you visit a GitHub Pages website, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes, whether or not you are signed in to GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. This hosting traffic is separate from local document processing in the Folio app.

9. Changes and contact

These documents describe the current offline app. A later version with different data practices needs a corresponding updated policy. The date at the top identifies this copy; downloaded offline copies do not update themselves.

Feedback and privacy requests: yfeiib@gmail.com.
