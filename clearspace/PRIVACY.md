# ClearSpace Privacy Policy

Updated October 25, 2026.

## Local processing

This policy applies to ClearSpace 1.0.0. Storage analysis stays on your Mac. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, remote crash reports, or cloud AI. ClearSpace does not upload files or scan results.

## What the app accesses

Requested scans read names, paths, sizes, file types, and filesystem metadata. App Caches checks running app identifiers. Quick Look reads a file only when you choose Preview File. Apple Intelligence downloads and macOS services follow your system settings and Apple's policies.

## Optional on-device AI

Only when requested, Apple's on-device model receives metadata for the current folder and up to five immediate subfolders, including sampled filenames. File contents are not included. AI responses stay in memory for the details session and cannot authorize deletion.

## Storage and removal

Scans stay in memory until the app closes. Up to 100 cleanup records, including dates, cache names, sizes, and Trash paths, remain in ~/Library/Containers/com.uniondream.cleanspace/Data/Library/Application Support/ClearSpace/history.json. Quit the app and delete that file to remove history. Reset preferences with: defaults delete ~/Library/Containers/com.uniondream.cleanspace/Data/Library/Preferences/com.uniondream.cleanspace. Uninstalling alone may leave this data. Trash contents are controlled by macOS. Language preferences and folder-access bookmarks stay in this app’s preferences until those preferences are reset.

## Voluntary feedback

Send Feedback opens your email app; nothing is sent automatically and no files or diagnostics are attached. If you send a message, ClearSpace support receives your address, message, and any attachments you choose. Your email provider also processes delivery. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to answer and resolve requests. Avoid sending sensitive files or unreviewed screenshots.

## Your choices and contact

You can stop scanning, skip AI, and remove local data as described above. For feedback, privacy questions, or deletion of support correspondence, contact yfeiib@gmail.com. Updated policies are bundled with future app versions; this copy works offline.
