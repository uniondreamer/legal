# Diskvero legal documents and support

Diskvero helps you understand Mac storage, explore folders, and review supported app caches. Scans and optional AI analysis run locally. Only confirmed selections from supported caches can move to Trash.

- [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md)
- [Terms of Use](TERMS.md)

For help with Diskvero, contact yfeiib@gmail.com. Describe the issue, your app and macOS versions, and steps to reproduce it. Remove personal filenames, home paths, and sensitive contents from screenshots. No files or diagnostics are sent automatically.

Standalone website files: [index.html](index.html), [privacy.html](privacy.html), and [terms.html](terms.html). Keep the three HTML files together when hosting them; all page links are relative.
